Our story

We make gear for the long way out

Since 2019

Started on a dirt road at dawn

SOLUM began with three runners, one workshop and a pile of jackets that had given up halfway through a season. We wanted layers that breathe on the climb, keep the wind off on the ridge, and last long enough to be passed on.

Everything we make is tested on the trails around our studio in the high desert, from summer heat to the first snow on the mesas. If it does not survive a year out there, it does not ship.

What we stand on

Recycled first

Shells, fleece and fill are made from recycled nylon and polyester, finished without PFCs.

Repairs for life

Rips, zips and worn cuffs: send it in and we fix it, for as long as you own it.

Carbon-neutral delivery

Every parcel's shipping emissions are measured and offset, and our packaging is paper only.
